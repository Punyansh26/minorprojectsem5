- About IIIT-NR Dr. Shyama Prasad Mukherjee International Institute of Information Technology, Naya Raipur (IIIT-NR) is a premier state-funded institute established in 2015 under the International Institute of Information Technology University Act, 2013 of the Government of Chhattisgarh. The institute is a joint initiative of the Government of Chhattisgarh and National Thermal Power Corporation (NTPC) Limited, a Maharatna Central Public Sector Undertaking under the Ministry of Power, Government of India. Incorporated as a state university and recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), IIIT-NR is committed to pursuing excellence in higher education, research, and development in Information Technology (IT) and associated disciplines. The institute firmly believes in knowledge dissemination and in imparting entrepreneurial skills to its students, enabling them to address real-world problems with confidence and creativity. The institute is housed on a fully residential, fifty-acre green campus in Naya Raipur (Atal Nagar) — India's first planned green and smart city — approximately 23 km from Raipur and well connected by air, rail, and road. The serene environment blends natural beauty with state-of-the-art infrastructure, creating an ideal space for innovation and learning. IIIT-NR offers undergraduate (B.Tech.), postgraduate (M.Tech.), and doctoral (Ph.D.) programmes in Computer Science and Engineering (CSE), Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE), and Data Science and Artificial Intelligence (DSAI). These credit-based programmes are designed to foster core competence together with innovation and entrepreneurship, with a dynamic curriculum structure that keeps pace with the changing needs of industry and academia. Vision of the Institute To be a globally recognized institution of excellence in information technology and allied disciplines, nurturing innovators, entrepreneurs, and socially responsible technologists who contribute to national and global development. B.Tech CSE Syllabus • Dr. SPM IIIT-NR 2. About the Department of CSE The Department of Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) at IIIT-NR offers a four-year Bachelor of Technology programme that builds a strong foundation in the theory and practice of computing — spanning programming, data structures and algorithms, systems, databases, networks, and the rapidly growing areas of artificial intelligence, machine learning, and data science. The programme combines rigorous fundamentals with extensive hands-on laboratory work, design projects, and industry-aligned skills so that graduates are equipped to design, build, and reason about complex software and computing systems. The department is taught by highly qualified and research-active faculty whose expertise spans artificial intelligence and machine learning, data science, computer networks and security, distributed and cloud systems, theoretical computer science, computer vision, and software engineering. Every semester integrates project-based learning, supported by well-equipped computing laboratories, high-performance computing resources, and the active mentorship of research scholars. AI-assisted full-stack prototyping is embedded from the first year, reflecting the institute's emphasis on innovation and entrepreneurship. Vision of the Department To emerge as a centre of excellence in computer science and engineering education and research, producing globally competent engineers, innovators, and entrepreneurs capable of solving complex computing problems for the benefit of society. Mission of the Department

- To provide a rigorous, outcome-based foundation in computer science and engineering with strong hands-on, design, and problem-solving exposure.
- To promote research, innovation, and entrepreneurship in emerging areas such as artificial intelligence, data science, cyber-security, and distributed and intelligent systems.
- To inculcate professional ethics, lifelong learning, and social responsibility among students.
- To build strong linkages with industry, research organizations, and the wider community for mutually beneficial collaboration. Programme Offered B.Tech. in Computer Science and Engineering — 4 years / 8 semesters, 160 credits, with multiple entry- exit options, multidisciplinary minors, an Honors track, and a strong project and internship component, in line with NEP 2020 and the NBA outcome-based education framework. B.Tech CSE Syllabus • Dr. SPM IIIT-NR 3. Outcome-Based Education (OBE) Framework The B.Tech. (CSE) curriculum is designed and delivered following the Outcome-Based Education (OBE) philosophy advocated by the National Board of Accreditation (NBA) and consistent with the Washington Accord. Under OBE, the curriculum, teaching-learning processes, and assessment are aligned to clearly defined outcomes at three levels — Programme Educational Objectives (PEOs), Programme Outcomes (POs) with Programme Specific Outcomes (PSOs), and Course Outcomes (COs). The OBE Hierarchy Outcome Level Description & Time-frame Broad statements describing the career and professional accomplishments PEOs graduates are expected to attain within 3–5 years of graduation. Statements of knowledge, skills, and attitudes students should possess at the time POs / PSOs of graduation; PSOs are programme-specific competencies (CSE). Specific, measurable abilities a student acquires on completing an individual COs course; COs are mapped to POs/PSOs and assessed each semester. Attainment is measured through direct assessment (continuous internal evaluation, end-semester examinations, laboratory and project rubrics) and indirect assessment (course-exit, programme-exit, alumni, and employer surveys). The closed feedback loop drives continuous quality improvement (CQI). 3.1 Programme Educational Objectives (PEOs) and Justification Within a few years of graduation, B.Tech.

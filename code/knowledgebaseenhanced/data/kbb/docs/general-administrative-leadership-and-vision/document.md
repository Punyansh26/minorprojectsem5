## Office of the Director

DIRECTOR

PROF. (DR.) OM PRAKASH VYAS DIRECTOR, IIIT NAYA RAIPUR

Prof. (Dr.) Om Prakash Vyas is a distinguished academician, researcher, and institutional leader
with over three decades of experience in technical education, research, and academic
administration. He holds an MJech. in Computer Science from IIT Kharagpur and pursued
doctoral research at Technical University of Kaiserslautern, Germany, under a DAAD Fellowship.

As the founding Head of Computer Science at Pt. Ravishankar Shukla University, he established
Chhattisgarh's first formal Computer Science programme in 1992. His early initiatives played a
pioneering role in extending Computer Science and IT education across a large network of
affiliated colleges in Chhattisgarh, helping lay the foundation for the State's subsequent expansion
of IT education.

Prof. Vyas has had a long and significant association with IlIT Allahabad, beginning as Assistant Professor during 2002-03, in the
formative years of the Institute. He returned to IlIT-A in 2009 as Professor of Information Technology and continued there until
2026, serving in several academic and leadership responsibilities, including as Dean (Research & Development), Dean (Technology
Development) and Member of BoG. During this period, he contributed to the Institute’'s academic development, including the
conceptualization and introduction of the BTech. programme in IT-Business Informatics in 2019, and coordinated NEP 2020-
inspired academic reforms for B.Tech. programs.

He has successfully led several national and international collaborative research projects and has been a sought-after Visiting
Professor at multiple global institutions. These include the National Institute of Informatics, Tokyo (Japan, 2010), the University of
Paderborn (Germany, 2013-2017), Inria Lille (France, 2015), the University of Messina (Italy, 2016), Norwegian Technical University
(Norway), and Bielefeld University (Germany, 2018-2023), where he taught and conducted research every summer for five
consecutive years.

As Vice Chancellor and Director of IlIT-Naya Raipur, Prof. Vyas has successfully implemented an NEP 2020-inspired BTech.
curriculum with a flexible Major-Minor structure, enabling students across engineering branches to pursue interdisciplinary Minors
including Business Informatics, Digital Mobility, Digital Humanities, Cyber Security, and Quantum Computing. His leadership
emphasizes research excellence, innovation, multidisciplinary education, and industry-academia and international collaboration.

His academic interests span Data Science, Process Intelligence, Business Informatics, Explainable Al, Cyber Security, and Agentic Al.
He is currently pursuing international research collaborations in Agentic Al through Indo-Italian initiatives and Cyber Security for
Critical Infrastructure through Indo-Norwegian projects.

## About IIIT Naya Raipur (Part A)

Dr. SPM lIIT-Naya Raipur, established by the International Institute of Information Technology University Act, 2013, enacted by the
Government of Chhattisgarh, is a joint venture by the Chhattisgarh State Government and National Thermal Power Corporation (NTPC), a
central Public Sector Undertaking (PSU) under the Ministry of Power, Government of India.The institute is committed to pursue excellence
in higher education, research and development in Information Technology (IT) and associated disciplines. It firmly believes in bestowing
knowledge dissemination and imparting entrepreneurial skills to the students to enable them to address real world problems. To
empower this vision, IIIT-NR fosters state-of-the-art research and product development laboratories to carry out inter-disciplinary research
and product development endeavours.The institute is mentored by a highly eminent board comprising of :-

Applies to: Semester 3 and Semester 4. Total Credits: Up to 32 credits (16 per semester)
Overview
All students must pursue a Thesis / Capstone Project / Industry Internship spanning Semesters 3 and 4.
Students must choose one of three tracks at the start of Semester 3:
 Research-Oriented Track
 Entrepreneurship-Oriented Track
 Industry Internship Track
Flexibility to Switch Track
Students are permitted to switch from one track to another after completion of Semester 3, subject to:
 Approval of the Academic Advisor / Thesis Committee
 Submission of a revised work plan
 Alignment with credit and evaluation expectations of the new track
 Upon switching, the evaluation in Semester 3 will be based on the original track, while Semester 4 will be
evaluated as per the new choice.

General Guidelines
 Students will be assigned an academic supervisor and, where applicable, an industry/startup mentor.
 Periodic reviews will be conducted during both semesters to ensure progress and accountability.
 Evaluation will follow track-specific rubrics, based on learning outcomes and final deliverables.
 Interdisciplinary problems, multi-student teams (for startups), and industry-collaboration are encouraged.

Electives are subject to change depending upon the availability of expertise, industry collaboration,

The institute retains the flexibility to:
 Revise or replace elective courses based on emerging technologies, evolving industry needs, and
faculty expertise.
 Offer electives in collaboration with industry partners, research labs, or through online platforms
(e.g., NPTEL, SWAYAM, Coursera) with appropriate academic integration.
 Allow students to take interdisciplinary electives from other departments or specializations, with
prior approval and after meeting prerequisites.
 Provide opportunities for guest lectures, micro-courses, workshops, and credit-bearing industry
projects in lieu of or alongside elective courses.
All changes will be communicated at the beginning of each semester through the academic office and
course planning committee.

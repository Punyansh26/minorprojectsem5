#### Brief Profile :

Dr. Sresha Yadav (née Ghosh) is an Associate Professor in the Department of Humanities. Before joining IIIT-NR, she served as an Assistant Professor at GITAM University, Bangalore. A UGC-NET qualified scholar, she was awarded the prestigious MHRD scholarship at IIT Roorkee during her doctoral studies. Her research has been widely disseminated through peer-reviewed journal articles and book chapters in publications indexed in SCI and MLA. She is an active member of several scholarly organisations, including the Digital Humanities Alliance for Research and Teaching Innovation (DHARTI), the Cognitive Science Society, ELTAI, Melus India, and the British Council.

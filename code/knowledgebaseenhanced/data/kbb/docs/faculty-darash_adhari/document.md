## Darash Adhari

#### Brief Profile :

Dr. Darash Adhari is a vocalist, researcher, composer, and academician specializing in Hindustani Classical Music. She earned her Ph.D. from Dayalbagh Educational Institute (DEI), Agra. She previously served as a Guest Faculty in the Department of Music at DEI. Dr. Darash is an accomplished composer with 50 original musical compositions to her credit. Her academic excellence and contributions to Indian music have been recognized through several prestigious honours, including the Sangeet Manishi Raghunath Talegaonkar Award & the International Women Achiever Award. Many students across Agra are receiving vocal training under her guidance and have achieved notable success in prestigious platforms such as the Uttar Pradesh Sangeet Natak Akademi (Lucknow), Yuva Mahotsav, Ninad Sangeet Mahotsav, and Sangeetotsav.

### Dhananjai Pandey(Chairperson)

A Professor Dhananjai Pandey is an internationally renowned educator and researcher in the field of Materials Science and Technology. His professional career started in 1977 as a Faculty Member in BHU/IIT (BHU) and he retired as Professor (HAG) in 2017 but continued as Institute Professor for another five years. He is presently a Distinguished Professor of BHU (for life) and IIT Bhilai.
A widely travelled scientist, Prof. Pandey has delivered Invited talks/Keynote addresses/ Plenary lectures at very large number of International and National Conferences held within India and abroad. He has published over 200 research articles in journals of international repute, books and book-series with over 8000 citations (14 papers with over 100 citations) and an h-index of 49. He also holds several patents to his credit. He is a Fellow of all the three premier science academies of India (Indian National Science Academy, Indian Academy of Sciences, National Academy of Sciences of India) and a Fellow of the Asia Pacific Academy of Materials. He is a recipient of several awards/medals (INSA Young Scientist Medal, Materials Research Society of India Medal, MRSI Annual Prize and MRSI Distinguished Lectureship award, Homi Bhabha Fellowship Award, DAE Raja Ramanna Award, Pt Jubilee Award of Indian Science Congress, J C Bose National Fellowship award etc). He has
He was Coordinator (Head) of the School of Materials Science and Technology at IT,BHU, first Director in the transition phase and Dean (Faculty Affairs) of IIT BHU. Under his leadership, the School of Mater Sci & Tech emerged as an Internationally reputed Centre of research and teaching in advanced functional materials. As a Dean of Faculty Affairs, policy documents for Salary fixation, Faculty Recruitment and Promotion of deserving existing faculty members as per IIT norms were developed and successfully implemented.

Professor Pandey has been Member of various committees of DST, CSIR, DRDO, UGC, INSA, IASc as well as Member of Governing Body/Council and Executive Council of IIT (BHU), BHU and SU, IUAC etc.
Professor Pandey has been Vice President and President of the Indian Crystallographic Association and Vice President of the Materials Research Society of India and is currently President of the Neutron Scattering Society of India.
Professor Pandey has served as Co-editor of the leading International journals published by the International Union of Crystallography, Acta Crystallographica Section A and Journal of Applied Crystallography (first Indian), and Member of Editorial Boards of International journals (Phase Transitions, Materials Science Forum and Zeitscrift-fur-kristallographie) as well as Indian journals (Bulletin of Materials Science and Praman-a Physics Journal). He presently serves on the editorial advisory boards of Zeitscrift-fur-kristallographie and Bulletin of Materials Science. He has served on many important international bodies like Ewald Prize Committee (the highest award in crystallography) and Asia-Oceania Neutron Scattering Association (AONSA) Prize Committee, Commission on Aperiodic Crystals, Commission on Inorganic Materials and Minerals, Commission on Mathematical and Theoretical Crystallography (MaThCryst), Sub-Committee on Nomenclature for Phase Transitions of the International Union of Crystallography (IUCr). He also served as Chairman of the MaThCryst Commission of the IUCr, being only the third Indian to be elected as Chairman of any one of the IUCr Commissions over the last seven decades. He is a founding Member of EC of AONSA and continues to be its Member from India at present.

### Giriraj Nyati

Dr. Giriraj Nyati is a professional with 40+ years of global experience in Engineering & Technology, Research, Operations, SCM, Strategy & Leadership. A PhD holder from IIT Delhi, with BE (Electrical & Electronics) from BITS, Pilani & MBA from Sukhadia University, Udaipur, Dr Nyati, brings on rich international experience of working with fortune 500 companies & Nobel laureates like Nakamura San. He was associated with Genus Power Infrastructure Limited & Secure Meters as COO & Moser Baer as CTO. Dr Nyati had been instrumental in joint envisioning & driving R&D projects related to Optical data storage, semiconductor & display technologies. He is credited with bringing technologies of strategic importance to country, setting up of highly advanced world class manufacturing operations & achieving less than 100 ppm defect level quality and reliability standards.
Dr Nyati is India’s one of the known technology experts in renewable energy & was the chairman of 5 government committees constituted by the Department of Electronics & Information technology related to nanotechnology based next generation solar cells. He is also the co -inventor of the “Light Scribe” technology & was awarded the FICCI best prize for “Technology & Innovation” in the year 2010 by then Finance minister, Mr Pranab Mukherjee. Dr Nyati had been instrumental in deploying a disruptive model for the benefits of the tribal belt of Rajasthan that resulted in the employment of over 1000 females from extremely poor strata of the society.

### Rajiv Prakash

Prof. Rajiv Prakash is the Director of IIT Bhilai. He was appointed to this position by the Hon’ble President of India in her capacity as the Visitor of IIT Bhilai. A Professor of Materials Science and Technology at IIT (BHU) Varanasi, Prof. Prakash has held several key academic and administrative responsibilities, including Head of the School of Materials Science and Technology (twice), Dean (R&D), founder coordinator of the Central Instrument Facility, and IPR Cell of the Institute.
He has made major translational contributions in sensor and biosensor engineering by integrating conducting polymers, nanomaterials, smartphone-based diagnostics, IoT-enabled sensing, and automated analysis. His technologies include field-deployable systems for arsenic detection in water, PSA-based cancer diagnostics, glucose sensing, and point-of-care platforms for kidney-related biomarkers and sickle-cell anaemia. Several of these technologies have reached advanced readiness levels and have been transferred to industry. His broader engineering contributions also include polymer composites for optical storage and railway applications, corrosion protection using conducting polymers and green inhibitors, charge-storage materials, and materials for green-energy applications.
Prof. Prakash has published more than 270 refereed journal papers (Google Scholar h-index of 53), holds 25 patents (with 3 technology transfer). His recognitions include the Young Scientist Award, INAE Young Engineer Award, MRSI Medal, election as Academician of the Asia Pacific Academy of Materials, and the Lifetime Engineering Excellence Award 2025 from The Institution of Engineers (India), Bhilai. As Director of IIT Bhilai and Chairman of IIT Bhilai Innovation and Technology Foundation (IBITF), a DST NM-ICPS Technology Innovation Hub, he has advanced innovation-led institutional growth. His recent leadership contributions include AI-enabled Vidya Samiksha Kendra initiatives for data-driven school governance in Chhattisgarh, the proposed IT Park in Durg, and the Tribal Research and Innovation Park (TRIP) in Bastar, which seeks to integrate deep-tech solutions with traditional knowledge for sustainable livelihood and regional development.

### Mukul S. Sutaone

Dr. Mukul Sutaone is currently the Director of Indian Institute of Information Technology, Allahabad (An Institute of National Importance with the Act of Parliament). In the immediate past, he graced the position of the First Vice Chancellor of COEP Technological University Pune, which is the legacy in the field of Engineering Education in the Country. He worked at College of Engineering, Pune (COEP) as Professor in Electronics and Telecommunication department for more than 30 years, with illustrious accomplishments in Academics and Research. At COEP, Prof. Sutaone handled various administrative responsibilities such as Officiating Director, Deputy Director, Dean Academics, Dean- International Relations, Head of the Department, etc.
His areas of work & interests in Teaching & Research are 'Statistical & Adaptive Signal Processing, Wireless Communication & Networks and Statistical Modeling of Data'. He has conceived, practiced, executed and steered many innovative academic experiments in curriculum design and delivery for the students and faculty of his Institute, Pune University and other aspiring institutes, mentored by him.
In the capacity of Principal investigator of the "Center of Excellence in Signal & Image Processing", a World Bank funded project by MHRD-GoI (under TEQIP), Prof. Sutaone has been associated with the development and conversion of prototypes from mere Proof of Concept (PoC) to transferrable technologies. Some of such projects are ‘Video Stabilization for Cell Phones’ ‘Automated Inspection System for PCB fault detection’, ‘Density and Direction detection for Stampedes’, and 'Optimal Microphone Array for Audio beam-forming'.
With handsome number of contributed and cited publications to his credit, a few granted patents and copyrights as well as multiple Govt./Corporate funded projects, he is an applauded resource person in many conferences, workshops and symposia, in the areas of Signal Processing, Next Generation Communication, Outcome based Education and Effective Deployment of New Education Policies. Prof. Sutaone is recipient of Prof. SVC Aiya IETE National Award for Excellence in Telecomm.

### Neeraj Jalota

Shri Neeraj Jalota is a senior power sector professional with over 37 years of distinguished experience encompassing project development, operations, maintenance, and coal mining. He holds an M.Tech in Industrial Engineering from IIT Delhi and a Post Graduate Diploma in Business Management from MDI Gurgaon, reflecting a strong blend of technical depth and managerial leadership.
Currently, Shri Jalota serves as the Executive Director, Unified Shared Services Centre (USSC), an integrated single-window platform responsible for materials procurement and contract management for NTPC stations nationwide. In this role, he has played a pivotal part in driving process standardization, operational efficiency, and enterprise-wide integration of shared services.
In addition, he holds the responsibility of Regional Executive Director (WR-II), as the administrative head of five major thermal power stations located at Korba, Sipat, and Lara in Chhattisgarh, and Gadarwara and Khargone in Madhya Pradesh. His mandate encompasses oversight of Operations and Maintenance activities for an aggregate operating capacity of 10,100 MW, as well as strategic supervision of ongoing capacity expansion projects totaling 4,000 MW, including 1,600 MW each at Lara and Gadarwara, and 800 MW at Sipat.

Prior to his current roles, Shri Jalota served as Chief Executive Officer of the Jhabua Thermal Power Plant, where he successfully transformed the captive power plant into a profitable operation, strengthened environmental compliance, and enhanced overall operational performance.
He has undergone several in-house and overseas training programmes in technical, managerial, and mining domains. Shri Jalota’s vast leadership experience, institutional perspective, and strategic insight contribute meaningfully to governance and capacity-building initiatives.

### Prof. (Dr.) Om Prakash Vyas Member Secretary

Prof. (Dr.) Om Prakash Vyas is a distinguished academician, researcher, and institutional leader with over three decades of experience in technical education, research, and academic administration. He holds an M.Tech. in Computer Science from IIT Kharagpur and pursued doctoral research at Technical University of Kaiserslautern, Germany, under a DAAD Fellowship.
As the founding Head of Computer Science at Pt. Ravishankar Shukla University, he established Chhattisgarh’s first formal Computer Science programme in 1992. His early initiatives played a pioneering role in extending Computer Science and IT education across a large network of affiliated colleges in Chhattisgarh, helping lay the foundation for the State’s subsequent expansion of IT education.
Prof. Vyas has had a long and significant association with IIIT Allahabad, beginning as Assistant Professor during 2002–03, in the formative years of the Institute. He returned to IIIT-A in 2009 as Professor of Information Technology and continued there until 2026, serving in several academic and leadership responsibilities, including as Dean (Research & Development), Dean (Technology Development) and Member of BoG. During this period, he contributed to the Institute’s academic development, including the conceptualization and introduction of the B.Tech. programme in IT–Business Informatics in 2019, and coordinated NEP 2020-inspired academic reforms for B.Tech. programs.
He has successfully led several national and international collaborative research projects and has been a sought-after Visiting Professor at multiple global institutions. These include the National Institute of Informatics, Tokyo (Japan, 2010), the University of Paderborn (Germany, 2013–2017), Inria Lille (France, 2015), the University of Messina (Italy, 2016), Norwegian Technical University (Norway), and Bielefeld University (Germany, 2018–2023), where he taught and conducted research every summer for five consecutive years.
As Vice Chancellor and Director of IIIT-Naya Raipur, Prof. Vyas has successfully implemented an NEP 2020-inspired B.Tech. curriculum with a flexible Major–Minor structure, enabling students across engineering branches to pursue interdisciplinary Minors including Business Informatics, Digital Mobility, Digital Humanities, Cyber Security, and Quantum Computing. His leadership emphasizes research excellence, innovation, multidisciplinary education, and industry–academia and international collaboration.
His academic interests span Data Science, Process Intelligence, Business Informatics, Explainable AI, Cyber Security, and Agentic AI. He is currently pursuing international research collaborations in Agentic AI through Indo-Italian initiatives and Cyber Security for Critical Infrastructure through Indo-Norwegian projects.

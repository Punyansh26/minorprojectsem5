## Lakhindar Murmu

#### Brief Profile :

Dr. Lakhindar Murmu is an Assistant Professor in Electronics and Communication Engineering discipline. Dr. Murmu published several articles in journals and conference proceedings of international repute. He has also served as reviewer of papers/articles of many reputed international journals and conferences. He received his Doctor of philosophy degree in the department of Electronics Engineering, Indian Institute of Technology (Indian School of Mines), Dhanbad, Jharkhand.

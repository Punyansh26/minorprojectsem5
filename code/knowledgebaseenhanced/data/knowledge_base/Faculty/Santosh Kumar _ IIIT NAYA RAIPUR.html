Santosh Kumar

Designation: (ASSOCIATE PROFESSOR, CSE)

Qualification: Ph.D (IIT B.H.U.)

Department: CSE

Email Id: santosh[at]iiitnr.edu.in

Contact No: 91-771-2474048

Brief Profile :

Dr. Santosh Kumar is an Assistant Professor in Computer Science and Engineering discipline. Prior to joining IIIT-NR, Dr. Santosh Kumar was a Ph.D. scholar in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, IIT (B.H.U.), Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh, India. He is an active  member of the Computer Society and the Association for Computing Machinery.
He has published over 14 journal (SCI-index journal) and 5 conference papers (including 2 tier-1 international conferences (ACM multimedia-2016 and Mobisys-2016); filed 1patent, and 14 book chapters in edited books (Springer publication and IGI publication).

 

Research Interest

Animal biometrics, Computer vision, Deep learning, Pattern recognition,Wireless sensors and Internet of Things (IoT).

Courses Offered

  • CS101I: IT Workshop-1: Autumn 2017, 2018
  • CSE211-Operating System,Spring 2017, Autumn 2018
  • CSE601-Database Management System and Warehousing (DBMSW), Spring 2018, Autumn 2018
  • CSE327 (OE)-Computer vision Spring, Autumn 2018, Spring 2019
  • CS109C-Web Programming, Spring 2019

Responsibilities

  • Member: RAC Committee

Professional Activities

Awards And Accolades

  •  Selected in top five finalists of received Best Doctoral Dissertation Award  during Institute Day–2016, I.I.T (B.H.U.), Varanasi-221005,
  • Received the “Best Researcher Award” in Doctoral Consortium at ACM conference on Mobisys’2016, Singapore.
  • Received National Level Scholarship Rajiv Gandhi National Fellowship (RGNF-2012 to 2017) based on academic/competitive examination performance in 2012.

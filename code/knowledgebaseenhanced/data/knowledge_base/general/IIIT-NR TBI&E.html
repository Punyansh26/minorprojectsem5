About us

Atal Incubation Centre incorporated under Section 8 of the Companies,

Act 2013 and promoted by IIIT Naya Raipur with an aim to promote innovation and

entrepreneurship in and around the state of Chhattisgarh. The incubatees will be supported

extensively through mentoring, access to knowledge and networking platforms, pitch sessions,

and exclusive access to IIIT Naya Raipur library and other facilities.

IIIT Naya Raipur has identified exclusive space of 12,000 sq ft for operating Atal Incubation

Centre with incubate workspaces, conference rooms, equipments, among others as detailed

below.

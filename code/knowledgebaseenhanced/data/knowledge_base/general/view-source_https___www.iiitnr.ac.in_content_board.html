<h3>Dhananjai Pandey<span>(Chairperson)</span></h3>
<p><img alt="" title="" class="" src="https://www.iiitnr.ac.in/newspace/photo/Chairman.jpg">
<p>A Professor Dhananjai Pandey is an internationally renowned educator and researcher in the field of Materials Science and Technology. His professional career started in 1977 as a Faculty Member in BHU/IIT (BHU) and he retired as Professor (HAG) in 2017 but continued as Institute Professor for another five years. He is presently a Distinguished Professor of BHU (for life) and IIT Bhilai. </p>
<p>A widely travelled scientist, Prof. Pandey has delivered Invited talks/Keynote addresses/ Plenary lectures at very large number of International and National Conferences held within India and abroad. He has published over 200 research articles in journals of international repute, books and book-series with over 8000 citations (14 papers with over 100 citations) and an h-index of 49. He also holds several patents to his credit. He is a Fellow of all the three premier science academies of India (Indian National Science Academy, Indian Academy of Sciences, National Academy of Sciences of India) and a Fellow of the Asia Pacific Academy of Materials. He is a recipient of several awards/medals (INSA Young Scientist Medal, Materials Research Society of India Medal, MRSI Annual Prize and MRSI Distinguished Lectureship award, Homi Bhabha Fellowship Award, DAE Raja Ramanna Award, Pt Jubilee Award of Indian Science Congress, J C Bose National Fellowship award etc). He has
He was Coordinator (Head) of the School of Materials Science and Technology at IT,BHU, first Director in the transition phase and Dean (Faculty Affairs) of IIT BHU. Under his leadership, the School of Mater Sci &amp; Tech emerged as an Internationally reputed Centre of research and teaching in advanced functional materials. As a Dean of Faculty Affairs, policy documents for Salary fixation, Faculty Recruitment and Promotion of deserving existing faculty members as per IIT norms were developed and successfully implemented.
</p>
<p>Professor Pandey has been Member of various committees of DST, CSIR, DRDO, UGC, INSA, IASc as well as Member of Governing Body/Council and Executive Council of IIT (BHU), BHU and SU, IUAC etc.</p>
<p>Professor Pandey has been Vice President and President of the Indian Crystallographic Association and Vice President of the Materials Research Society of India and is currently President of the Neutron Scattering Society of India.</p>
<p>Professor Pandey has served as Co-editor of the leading International journals published by the International Union of Crystallography, Acta Crystallographica Section A and Journal of Applied Crystallography (first Indian), and Member of Editorial Boards of International journals (Phase Transitions, Materials Science Forum and Zeitscrift-fur-kristallographie) as well as Indian journals (Bulletin of Materials Science and Praman-a Physics Journal). He presently serves on the editorial advisory boards of Zeitscrift-fur-kristallographie and Bulletin of Materials Science. He has served on many important international bodies like Ewald Prize Committee (the highest award in crystallography) and Asia-Oceania Neutron Scattering Association (AONSA) Prize Committee, Commission on Aperiodic Crystals, Commission on Inorganic Materials and Minerals, Commission on Mathematical and Theoretical Crystallography (MaThCryst), Sub-Committee on Nomenclature for Phase Transitions of the International Union of Crystallography (IUCr). He also served as Chairman of the MaThCryst Commission of the IUCr, being only the third Indian to be elected as Chairman of any one of the IUCr Commissions over the last seven decades. He is a founding Member of EC of AONSA and continues to be its Member from India at present.</p>
</div>

<h3>Giriraj Nyati </h3>
<p><img alt="" title="" class="" src="https://www.iiitnr.ac.in/newspace/photo/brnew2.jpg">
<p>Dr. Giriraj Nyati is a professional with 40+ years of global experience in Engineering &amp; Technology, Research, Operations, SCM, Strategy &amp; Leadership. A PhD holder from IIT Delhi, with BE (Electrical &amp; Electronics) from BITS, Pilani &amp; MBA from Sukhadia University, Udaipur, Dr Nyati, brings on rich international experience of working with fortune 500 companies &amp; Nobel laureates like Nakamura San. He was associated with Genus Power Infrastructure Limited &amp; Secure Meters as COO &amp; Moser Baer as CTO. Dr Nyati had been instrumental in joint envisioning &amp; driving R&amp;D projects related to Optical data storage, semiconductor &amp; display technologies. He is credited with bringing technologies of strategic importance to country, setting up of highly advanced world class manufacturing operations &amp; achieving less than 100 ppm defect level quality and reliability standards.</p>
<p>Dr Nyati is India’s one of the known technology experts in renewable energy &amp; was the chairman of 5 government committees constituted by the Department of Electronics &amp; Information technology related to nanotechnology based next generation solar cells. He is also the co -inventor of the “Light Scribe” technology &amp; was awarded the FICCI best prize for “Technology &amp; Innovation” in the year 2010 by then Finance minister, Mr Pranab Mukherjee. Dr Nyati had been instrumental in deploying a disruptive model for the benefits of the tribal belt of Rajasthan that resulted in the employment of over 1000 females from extremely poor strata of the society.
</p>

<h3>Rajiv Prakash</h3>
<p><img alt="" title="" class="" src="https://www.iiitnr.ac.in/newspace/photo/brnew3.jpg">
<p>Prof. Rajiv Prakash is the Director of IIT Bhilai. He was appointed to this position by the Hon’ble President of India in her capacity as the Visitor of IIT Bhilai. A Professor of Materials Science and Technology at IIT (BHU) Varanasi, Prof. Prakash has held several key academic and administrative responsibilities, including Head of the School of Materials Science and Technology (twice), Dean (R&amp;D), founder coordinator of the Central Instrument Facility, and IPR Cell of the Institute.</p>



<p>He has made major translational contributions in sensor and biosensor engineering by integrating conducting polymers, nanomaterials, smartphone-based diagnostics, IoT-enabled sensing, and automated analysis. His technologies include field-deployable systems for arsenic detection in water, PSA-based cancer diagnostics, glucose sensing, and point-of-care platforms for kidney-related biomarkers and sickle-cell anaemia. Several of these technologies have reached advanced readiness levels and have been transferred to industry. His broader engineering contributions also include polymer composites for optical storage and railway applications, corrosion protection using conducting polymers and green inhibitors, charge-storage materials, and materials for green-energy applications.</p>

<p>Prof. Prakash has published more than 270 refereed journal papers (Google Scholar h-index of 53), holds 25 patents (with 3 technology transfer). His recognitions include the Young Scientist Award, INAE Young Engineer Award, MRSI Medal, election as Academician of the Asia Pacific Academy of Materials, and the Lifetime Engineering Excellence Award 2025 from The Institution of Engineers (India), Bhilai. As Director of IIT Bhilai and Chairman of IIT Bhilai Innovation and Technology Foundation (IBITF), a DST NM-ICPS Technology Innovation Hub, he has advanced innovation-led institutional growth. His recent leadership contributions include AI-enabled Vidya Samiksha Kendra initiatives for data-driven school governance in Chhattisgarh, the proposed IT Park in Durg, and the Tribal Research and Innovation Park (TRIP) in Bastar, which seeks to integrate deep-tech solutions with traditional knowledge for sustainable livelihood and regional development.


</p></div>

<h3>Mukul S. Sutaone </h3>
<p><img alt="" title="" class="" src="https://www.iiitnr.ac.in/newspace/photo/mss.jpg">
<p>Dr. Mukul Sutaone is currently the Director of Indian Institute of Information Technology, Allahabad (An Institute of National Importance with the Act of Parliament). In the immediate past, he graced the position of the First Vice Chancellor of COEP Technological University Pune, which is the legacy in the field of Engineering Education in the Country. He worked at College of Engineering, Pune (COEP) as Professor in Electronics and Telecommunication department for more than 30 years, with illustrious accomplishments in Academics and Research. At COEP, Prof. Sutaone handled various administrative responsibilities such as Officiating Director, Deputy Director, Dean Academics, Dean- International Relations, Head of the Department, etc.</p>
<p>His areas of work &amp; interests in Teaching &amp; Research are 'Statistical &amp; Adaptive Signal Processing, Wireless Communication &amp; Networks and Statistical Modeling of Data'. He has conceived, practiced, executed and steered many innovative academic experiments in curriculum design and delivery for the students and faculty of his Institute, Pune University and other aspiring institutes, mentored by him.</p>

<p>In the capacity of Principal investigator of the "Center of Excellence in Signal &amp; Image Processing", a World Bank funded project by MHRD-GoI (under TEQIP), Prof. Sutaone has been associated with the development and conversion of prototypes from mere Proof of Concept (PoC) to transferrable technologies. Some of such projects are ‘Video Stabilization for Cell Phones’ ‘Automated Inspection System for PCB fault detection’, ‘Density and Direction detection for Stampedes’, and 'Optimal Microphone Array for Audio beam-forming'.
With handsome number of contributed and cited publications to his credit, a few granted patents and copyrights as well as multiple Govt./Corporate funded projects, he is an applauded resource person in many conferences, workshops and symposia, in the areas of Signal Processing, Next Generation Communication, Outcome based Education and Effective Deployment of New Education Policies. Prof. Sutaone is recipient of Prof. SVC Aiya IETE National Award for Excellence in Telecomm.
</p></div>

<h3>Ankit Anand</h3>
<p><img alt="" title="" class="" src="https://www.iiitnr.ac.in/newspace/photo/ias.jpg">

# Faculty Profile: Charu Krishna

Designation: (Faculty on Contract)

Department: CSE

Email Id: charu@iiitnr.edu.in

She earned her Ph.D. degree from Motilal Nehru National Institute of Technology Allahabad, Prayagraj and her Master’s degree from Delhi Technological University. Her Ph.D. thesis title is "Design of Blockchain Enabled Healthcare System using Convergence of Machine Learning and IoT Framework". She acts as reviewer in various reputed journals.

## Shubhangi Nema

#### Brief Profile :

Prior to joining IIIT-NR, I served as a Principal Project Scientist at IIT Delhi, where I worked on a research project funded by the Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR), New Delhi. I received my Ph.D. in Systems and Control Engineering from IIT Bombay. I am also a recipient of the prestigious Prime Minister’s Research Fellowship (PMRF), awarded to outstanding research scholars pursuing doctoral research at Centrally Funded Technical Institutes (CFTIs). I actively contribute to the research community as a reviewer for several IEEE and IFAC conferences and leading journals published by Springer and Wiley and invited for talks in many prestigious institutes of national importance.

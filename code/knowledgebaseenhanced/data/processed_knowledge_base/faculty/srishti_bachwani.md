## Srishti Bachwani

#### Brief Profile :

Dr. Srishti Bachwani is an Assistant Professor at the International Institute of Information Technology, Naya Raipur (IIIT-NR). She holds a Ph.D. in Marketing from the Indian Institute of Management Lucknow, an MBA in Marketing, and a Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science Engineering. Her interdisciplinary academic background enables her to integrate perspectives from technology, management, and consumer behaviour in her teaching and research. Her research has been published in international peer-reviewed journals and presented at leading academic conferences. Dr. Bachwani is particularly interested in interdisciplinary research that connects technology and business, with a focus on understanding how emerging digital technologies shape markets, organizations, and consumer decision-making.

# Faculty Profile: Khilesh Kumar

Designation: (Faculty on Contract)

Qualification: Ph. D. (Yoga Therapy for Diabetes)

Department: SHM

Email Id: khileshkumar@iiitnr.edu.in

Dr. Khilesh Kumar is a Yoga Expert, Researcher, Counselor, and Yoga Therapist. He earned his Ph.D. in Yoga from Dev Sanskriti Vishwavidyalaya (DSVV), Sankra, Durg, Chhattisgarh. He previously served as a Yoga Expert at Arogya Mandir Wellness Center, Raipur, and is currently working as an Adjunct Faculty at the International Institute of Information Technology (IIIT), Naya Raipur.
His areas of professional expertise include Asana and Pranayama, Meditation, Surya Namaskar, Mudra, Bandha, Shat Kriya, Stress Management, Counseling, and Yoga Therapy. His professional approach focuses on the integrated application of Yoga and wellness practices to promote physical, mental, and psychological well-being.

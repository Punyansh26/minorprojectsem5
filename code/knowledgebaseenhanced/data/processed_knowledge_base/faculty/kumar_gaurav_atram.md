## Kumar Gaurav Atram

#### Brief Profile :

Kumar Gaurav Atram is Professor of Practice at IIIT Naya Raipur in the Department of Data Science and Artificial Intelligence. He holds a Masters degree from University of St Andrews, UK. His research interests include Machine Learning, Deep Learning and Generative AI. Mr. Atram has 20+ year of Industry experience working with organisations like JP Morgan, TCS, Debenhams.

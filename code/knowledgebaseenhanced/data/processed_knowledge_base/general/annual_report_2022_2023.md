The Chancellor
Hon'ble Governor of Chhattisgarh, Shri Biswabhusan Harichandan, is the Chancellor of
Dr. Shyama Prasad Mukherjee International Institute of Technology - Naya Raipur (IIIT-NR).
He assumed the office of the Governor on February 23, 2023. Before serving as the Governor
of Chhattisgarh, he was the Governor of the State of Andhra Pradesh.
Shri Harichandan is an ardent democrat in all respect who fights for human rights,
democracy, democratic values, and citizens’ rights, especially rights of down trodden people
and against corruption. He has an enriching political career. He was elected to the Odisha State
Legislative Assembly five times. He has served people in various capacities including Revenue
Minister, Law Minister, Industry Minister in the then Odisha Government. He also served as
Cabinet Minister in 1977 (during emergency), and as the Chairman of the Cabinet Sub-
Committee on Rehabilitation and Re-settlement of the displaced persons which was then the
best Rehabilitation and Re-settlement Policy of the Country.
Shri Harichandan is also a reputed columnist who has written several articles on
contemporary political issues, historical, social and cultural matters, which have been published
in all leading newspapers of Odisha and some English weeklies of Delhi. He is an inspiring
author and has significant literary contributions to his name, including his autobiography-
‘Battle Not Yet Over’, which is focussed on his struggle in political, administrative, social,
cultural and other fields during his long public life.

Director’s Note
It’s indeed a privilege to present the Annual Report
2022-23 of this rapidly growing IIIT-Naya Raipur.
About 8 years ago, IIIT-NR embarked upon its journey
to realize the State Government’s vision to create a
premier technical institute of the country in Chhattisgarh.
I am proud to report that “Team IIIT-NR” has
successfully converted this dream into reality. With an
annual intake of only 80 students in two B. Tech.
programmes in 2015, today IIIT-NR offers three B. Tech.
programmes, four M. Tech. disciplines, multi-
disciplinary M. S. (by Research) and Ph. D. programmes,
with a total annual intake of 230 students. These
programmes cover a wide range of state-of-the-art
technology disciplines.
Our industry-oriented teaching-learning pedagogy helped achieve 100% placements in the first
four consecutive years, with an average package higher than parallel institutes. Some of our key
campus recruiters include Microsoft Research, American Express, Zeiss, Apra Labs, Big
Basket, Cognizant, Lumiq, Infosys, KPMG, Capgemini, PharmEasy, Ericsson, Deloitte etc.. In
addition, students interested in higher studies secured admissions in renowned academic
institutes both in India and abroad. These include IIT Bombay, IIT Delhi, University of
Massachusetts Amherst (USA), University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (USA), Cornell
University (USA), and Carnegie Mellon University (USA).
Team IIIT-NR firmly believes that both education and research are equally important for a
premier Higher Education Institute (HEI). Hence, just like innovative academic programmes,
IIIT-NR took a unique approach to carve out its research landscape. It decided to make IIIT-NR
“A Living Research Lab for Agriculture, Forestry and Rural Development”, by focusing on
applications of ICET (Information, Communication and Electronics Technologies) for
agriculture, forestry and rural development. Being situated in Chhattisgarh, a state rich in
agriculture and forestry, and dominated by rural population, we chose to focus on these areas
so that we can effectively contribute to the inclusive development of the state. I am proud to
report that IIIT-NR is currently pursuing several funded research projects in these areas which
have resulted in the publication of 242 journal papers, 18 book chapters, 4 books, and 7 patents
in the year 2022-23. Our faculty members also conducted several Faculty Development
Programs (funded by AICTE) in futuristic technology domains like Robotics, Artificial
Intelligence, Data Science, etc.

With its firm belief in advancing society through multidisciplinary collaborative research, IIIT-
NR has signed MoUs with several premier academic and research institutes. These include All

institute and the State of Chhattisgarh are gaining increased popularity nationally and
internationally as a research hub.
Through its entrepreneurship initiatives, IIIT-NR also motivates and nurtures its students to
become job creators as an entrepreneur. The institute received the ‘Start Me Up Award’ by
Wadhwani Foundation for its entrepreneurship initiatives and active participation in global
entrepreneurship competitions. Recognizing the new wave where startups will be the dominant
job creators of the future, the institute is also setting up an incubation facility in the campus.
The institute got 3.5-Star rating by the Institution Innovation Council (IIC), Ministry of
Education (MoE) and ‘Peformers Band’ in Atal Rankings of Institutions on Innovation
Achievements (ARIIA).
To develop the sense of social responsibility in its members, IIIT-NR has established ISR
(Institute Social Responsibility) Cell and NSS (National Social Service) unit in its campus.
Through these units, IIIT-NR members help less privileged sections of our society in various
ways. For example, the institute has adopted two nearby villages (Rakhi and Uparwara) where
it conducts special classes for village school children; conducts cleanliness and awareness
programmes for the villagers; undertakes projects for tribal and rural development; conducts
invited talks for members of other technical institutes to share the best practices of IIIT-NR;
conducts summer camps for school children; conducts an annual event called ‘SCIƞTFIC’
(Science and Technology over Fiction) to motivate school children towards career in Science
and Technology etc.
In addition to our students excelling globally in various ways as mentioned above, several
faculty members received international recognitions like selection/ nomination in executive
positions in professional societies, elevation to IEEE Senior Member Grade, Gold Faculty
Award by Wadhwani Foundation, etc. One of our faculty, Dr. Ruhul Amin, got listed for two
consecutive years, among the top 2% most-cited scientists globally in the list released by
Stanford University, USA.
To sum up, 2022-23 has been a year of success and accelerated growth of IIIT-NR. I
acknowledge that the success and the brand that IIIT-NR has earned in this short span would
not have been possible without the wholehearted support of Govt. of Chhattisgarh and NTPC
and our visionary Board members for their guidance and support in all matters. I extend my
sincere thanks to all stakeholders including the brilliant efforts of talented students, academic
accomplishments of our faculty members and committed staff.

About the Institute
Dr. Shyama Prasad Mukherjee International Institute of Information Technology – Naya Raipur
(IIIT-NR) is a joint venture by Govt. of Chhattisgarh and National Thermal Power Corporation
(NTPC). The institute is established by the International Institute of Information Technology
University Act, 2013 of the Govt. of Chhattisgarh “to promote and facilitate studies, research,
incubation and extension work in Information Technology and in its application domain and
related areas of knowledge and to achieve excellence in these fields and matters connected
therewith or incidental thereto.”
As enshrined in the Act, IIIT-Naya Raipur is committed to pursue excellence in higher
education, research and development in Information Technology (IT) and associated
disciplines. It firmly believes in bestowing knowledge dissemination and imparting
entrepreneurial skills to the students to enable them to address real world problems. To empower
this vision, IIIT-NR fosters state-of-the-art research and product development laboratories to
carry out inter-disciplinary research and product development endeavours. The institute is
mentored by a highly eminent board comprising of industry leaders, erudite academicians and
administrators.

IIIT-NR Campus and Location
IIIT-NR's fifty-acre fully residential campus is located in the smart city of Nava Raipur, Atal
Nagar approximately 23 Kms from Raipur - the capital city of Chhattisgarh state. Raipur is one
of the unique cities where various Institutions of National Importance (INIs) and premier
academic institutions like IIT, IIM, AIIMS, NIT, HNLU, PRSU, IGKV and IIIT-NR are located
in close proximity. Availability of these premier higher educational and research organisations
at one place makes the city a perfect educational hub. It gives students and scholars a perfect
platform with plenty of opportunities
and appropriate ambience and
ecosystem for teaching, learning and
research on wide spectrum of subjects.
The city is well connected by air, rail
and road to all parts of the country.
The vibrant lush green campus of IIIT-
NR is enabled with Wi-Fi connectivity,

IIIT-NR Logo
Description of Logo
The logo of Dr. Shyama Prasad Mukherjee IIIT-Naya Raipur
is designed to capture the Institute’s vision towards its social
responsibility along with its name and location. The logo
indicates that our institute is located in the State of
Chhattisgarh and provides an environment to foster
knowledge enrichment and development of humane qualities
to enable individuals to emerge as enlightened citizens, who
can lead a peaceful and contended life. The various
components of the logo signify the following:
Buddha: “Buddha” signifies enlightenment (an ideal state of
intellectual and ethical perfection) through gain of knowledge,
discipline, dedication and concentration. This object in the logo
indicates that DSPM-IIIT Naya Raipur nurtures such qualities in its
students and other members.
Circle Around Buddha: This artistically designed circle has both the
letters ‘C’ and ‘G’ embedded, signifying Chhattisgarh. This object in
the logo indicates that DSPM-IIIT Naya Raipur is located in and is
supported by the State of Chhattisgarh.
Sanskrit Shloka: Taken from Bhagavad Gita
(4:39), it means that ultimate wisdom and
peace in life can be achieved only by
acquiring knowledge. This object in the logo indicates that DSPM-IIIT Naya Raipur strives hard
to impart as much knowledge as possible to its students and other members to help them lead a
peaceful and contended life.
Institute Name in Long and Short
Forms: The logo also contains the institute’s
name in both long and short forms.

IIIT-NR Vision and Mission
Vision
To be a leading technology institute that produces high quality IT professionals and fosters
technological innovation for social upliftment.
Mission
To create an Institute of Excellence in IT Education and Research for imparting knowledge
through education, advancing knowledge through research, and providing service to the region,
nation and mankind to contribute towards the creation of a better society.
Vision 2.0
The first five-year focus of the institute was to properly establish its education and culture
components. The next five year vision (IIIT-NR 2.0) focuses on strengthening the research
component of the institute with a motto to transform the institute into “A Living Research Lab
for Agriculture, Forestry and Rural Development” by focusing on the application of ICET
(Information, Communication and Electronics Technologies) for agriculture, forestry, and rural
development.

Labs @ IIIT-NR
To provide students with hands-on experience for strong practical and technical skills with focus
on real world problems and also to facilitate quality teaching, learning, and research, the IIIT-
NR has setup various state-of-the-art labs. These are as follows:-
 The 3D Printing & Fabrication Lab
The 3D Printing and Fabrication Lab is a state-of-the-art digital manufacturing space. It allows
students to quickly fabricate parts from computer generated designs to rapidly iterate prototype
designs.
 VLSI Embedded System Lab
VLSI Embedded System Lab is well equipped with industry standard VLSI tools and hardware
resources like Raspberry PI board, Universal FPGA/CPLD Card, XILINX FPGA -ADD ON
Card, XILINX FPGA ZYNQ, etc. The primary objective of the lab is to provide hands-on
experience to students in the field of analog, digital and mixed-signal circuit design so that they
can conduct world class research, produce competitive results, create solutions tailored to
society's needs, and to disseminate knowledge for public use.
 BigData Computing Lab
BigData Computing Lab enables training and research in all aspects of large and distributed
information systems (e.g. system development, testing, maintenance, data integration,
networking, and application development). It prepares students for emerging cutting-edge
technologies in the fast-growing IT industry such as BigData Computing, Healthcare
Informatics, Data Integration and Data Analytics.
 Internet of Things & Sensors Lab
IoT Lab is at the forefront of developing both hardware, including novel sensors,
communication platform and analytics for Internet of Things (IoT) and smart environments. The
IoT lab provides necessary software and equipment to develop and characterize novel smart
sensors for IoT applications. Along with these, the lab is equipped with various kits like Smart
Healthcare, Smart Agriculture, Smart Village/ City.
 Network Lab
Fully equipped with Dell PCs and Netsim, Ns2 Simulators, the Network lab enables students to
conduct research in the area of wired and wireless networks. The lab also emphasizes
experimental research in the area of communication networking.
 Artificial Intelligence & Deep Learning (AIDL) Lab
The AIDL Lab enables the students to carry out experiments and research on a variety of topics
within Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning with focus on using real world problems to
discover computationally hard problems of modelling uncertainty, learning predictive models
and enabling decision making.

 Communication Lab
The Communication Lab provides necessary hardware and software resources to the
undergraduate and research students of Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) for
enhancing their skills in the communication field. Theory in the classroom is translated directly
into practice with the help of the Ettus USRP software defined radio platform and
Matlab/National Instruments LabVIEW. The emphasis is on physical layer concepts rather than
implementation considerations.
 Electronic Circuit Lab
The primary objective of this Lab is to provide engineering skills by way of breadboard circuit
design with electronic devices and components and to analyse various electronic circuits and
digital circuits such as multi-vibrators, Flip-Flop circuits, applications of operational amplifiers,
RC coupled amplifiers, oscillators, etc. so that students are able to understand the practical
aspects of basic electronics theory. It also enables the students to simulate and test the Analog,
Digital and mixed electronics circuits using Verilog, Matlab and OrCAD software.
 Computer Programming Lab
Computer Programming Lab is a hands-on project and assignment-based learning space where
students can gain strong practical and technical skills in various programming languages,
including C, Python, Java, UNIX/Linux: shell, tools, utilities and programming environments,
user interfaces, and software engineering principles.
 Antenna & Microwave Circuit Design Lab
This Lab supports intermediate and advanced courses in Electromagnetics and Microwave
Engineering. The microwave laboratories provide the necessary support for training the students
in the area of RF and Microwave Engineering. It offers analysis and simulation of various
components and devices to understand the basics of RF and microwave engineering, to boost
the quality of engineering education, deepen understanding, and provide the necessary practical
skills to young minds.
 Data Science Lab
Data Science Lab is configured to not only facilitate students to practice basic programming in
C, C++ and JAVA; but also to learn advanced technologies of computer science such as
Machine Learning, Artificial Intelligence, Deep Learning and Data Mining. The Lab facilitates
database designing and management through dedicated tools such as Oracle and MySQL, and
related computing paradigm and frameworks.
 Language Lab
The blended methodology used at the Language Laboratory caters to the language needs of
students by providing them suitable environment for self-paced effective language learning. The
primary objective of the lab is to enhance language learning skills of the students based on their
individual requirements. The lab is well-equipped with Digital Language Learning Software
platform that aims to facilitate efficient teacher-student interaction to correct phonetic accents
and dialects.

RULES FOR ALLOTMENT OF GUEST HOUSE ACCOMMODATION
The Institute Guest House currently offers 17 deluxe rooms, 3 suites, and a dining area. The institute has
well-defined rules for allocating guest house accommodations. Accommodation is available for guests
visiting on official and personal purposes with varying tariffs. Out of three suites, one has been designated
as the Chairman’s suite. The Chairman’s suite can only be allotted with the permission of the Vice-
Chancellor/Director.
Official Guest- means those person(s) invited by way of a written communication/mail/fax by the
designated authority, i.e. VC & Director/Registrar/Dean, to attend official engagements in the institute.
Non-official Guest- means a person visiting an employee (Teaching and Non-teaching)/students of the
institute for personal purposes.
Exclusions- Persons in the employment of the institute are not construed as guests. However, such a
person may be permitted to avail of the facility available to guests on payment of the requisite charges and
upon written orders of the competent authority, i.e., the Registrar/Director.
1. A person coming to join the institute on their/first appointment other than from Raipur/ Naya Raipur
may be permitted to stay in the guest house for a period of up to 15 days free of cost. Further stay shall
draw charges of Rs. 2730.00/month. The maximum stay period in the guest house for new joiners shall be
limited to 45 days.
2. The guests are allowed to stay in the guest house for the officially approved duration of the stay.
3. No charges on account of boarding and lodging shall be levied on the official guests.
4. There is no fixed check-in time. However, for accounting purposes, a day is defined as 24 hours from
the time of check-in.
5. Booking of the accommodation shall not confer on the allotee any right to tenancy of the premises, and
the institute has the right 10 get the room vacated at any time without assigning any time or reason 11 case
of unauthorised stay/or stay.
6. The guest house will be under the administrative control of the designated authority, as appointed by the
Vice-Chancellor/Director. The guest is expected to maintain harmony and good behaviour throughout their
stay.
7. No unauthorised person shall be allowed to stay in the guest house.
8. The institute shall not be responsible for any loss or damage to a person or the property of the guest
during his/her stay in the guest house.
9. Persons using the guest house will pay for the damages caused by them to the building, fixtures or
appliances.
10. For non-official guests, the request for an accommodation will be accepted only on recommendation
from faculty members or officers.

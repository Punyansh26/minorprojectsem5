# Vocational Training Programme

45 Days Vocational Training/Internship Program-2026 at IIIT Naya Raipur

IIIT Naya Raipur is pleased to announce a 45-days summer vocational training/internship program in physical mode at IIIT Naya Raipur campus during summer break of 2026. It will be a professionally designed training program aimed to enhance the employability and research skills among UG/PG students. It will be driven by the expert faculties of IIIT Naya Raipur and their collaborating industry partners (wherever available) to give students exposure to emerging technologies and make them industry-ready.

Here is the more details about the program:

- Target Audience: B.Tech. /M.Tech. /MBA /M.Com. /MCA
- Duration: 6-8 Weeks
- Mode: Physical Mode
- Enrolment/Registration Fee: 8260/- (Reg fee 7000+ 18% GST)
- Enrolment Link: https://forms.gle/G6jEaxdF3evFC7ne9 NOTE: The registration fee covers the course logistics and certification. For accommodation, hostel rooms are available on campus at Rs50 per day or Rs1500 for the whole VT duration (45 days). Canteen/mess is available for food on a pay-and-eat basis.

Timelines (tentative):

Start of the Program:

- In view the different exam schedules of different participating institutions, the vocational training (VT-2026) at IIIT Naya Raipur is planned in two Phases for different category of students:
- Phase-I: June 8-July 22, 2026 (For VI semester students of CSVTU affiliated institutions + Non-CSVTU-affiliated institution's students)
- Phase-II (Tentative): June 26- August 9, 2026 (For IV Sem students of CSVTU affiliated institutions + Non-CSVTU-affiliated institution's students, may change as per CSVTU exam schedule for IV Sem students)

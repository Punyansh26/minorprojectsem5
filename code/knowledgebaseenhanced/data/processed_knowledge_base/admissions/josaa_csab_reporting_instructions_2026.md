### Important Notes

1. After completing physical reporting, candidates may either return home or stay in a temporarily allotted room in one
of the hostels until final hostel allotment is completed for all first-year students. During this period, meals will be
available in the canteen on a payment basis.
2. The Academic Session will commence on 20th August 2026.
3. Each first-year student will be provided a double occupancy room with a bed, a study table, and a chair in the hostel.
4. For personal shopping needs, a local market is available at Sector 27, approximately 2 km from the campus.

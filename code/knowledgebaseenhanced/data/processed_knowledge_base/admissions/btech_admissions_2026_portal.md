Admission into the B. Tech programs can be through two categories:

- All-India Quota*: 35% of seats are reserved for the All-India candidates, and these seats are filled through the JoSAA 2025 counselling. You can visit the JoSAA 2026 website for more details.
- CG  & NTPC Quotas*: 50% of seats are reserved for candidates who have passed 10th & 12th from the State of Chhattisgarh, and 15% of seats are reserved for candidates employed with NTPC or their wards. These seats are filled through the IIIT-NR Online counselling process.
Admissions are strictly based on the merit of the candidates as inferred from their performance in JEE (Main) 2026. The eligibility for applying for the B. Tech programs under the CG quota at IIIT Naya Raipur is 50% aggregate marks in Chemistry, Physics, and Mathematics (45% for SC/ST/OBC/PWD candidates) in their 12th standard examination.  The eligibility for applying for the B. Tech programs under the All India category and the NTPC quota are as per JoSAA 2026 business rules. Please read the B Tech Admission Brochure 2026 for more details.

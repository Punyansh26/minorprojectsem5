1. About IIIT-NR
Dr. Shyama Prasad Mukherjee International Institute of Information Technology, Naya Raipur (IIIT-NR) is
a premier state-funded institute established in 2015 under the International Institute of Information
Technology University Act, 2013 of the Government of Chhattisgarh. The institute is a joint initiative of the
Government of Chhattisgarh and National Thermal Power Corporation (NTPC) Limited, a Maharatna
Central Public Sector Undertaking under the Ministry of Power, Government of India.
Incorporated as a state university and recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and
approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), IIIT-NR is committed to pursuing
excellence in higher education, research, and development in Information Technology (IT) and associated
disciplines. The institute firmly believes in knowledge dissemination and in imparting entrepreneurial skills
to its students, enabling them to address real-world problems with confidence and creativity.
The institute is housed on a fully residential, fifty-acre green campus in Naya Raipur (Atal Nagar) — India's
first planned green and smart city — approximately 23 km from Raipur and well connected by air, rail, and
road. The serene environment blends natural beauty with state-of-the-art infrastructure, creating an ideal
space for innovation and learning.
IIIT-NR offers undergraduate (B.Tech.), postgraduate (M.Tech.), and doctoral (Ph.D.) programmes in
Computer Science and Engineering (CSE), Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE), and Data
Science and Artificial Intelligence (DSAI). These credit-based programmes are designed to foster core
competence together with innovation and entrepreneurship, with a dynamic curriculum structure that keeps
pace with the changing needs of industry and academia.
Vision of the Institute
To be a globally recognized institution of excellence in information technology and allied disciplines,
nurturing innovators, entrepreneurs, and socially responsible technologists who contribute to national and
global development.

2. About the Department of ECE
The Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) at IIIT-NR offers a four-year
Bachelor of Technology programme that provides a strong conceptual foundation in electronics,
communication, signal processing, and VLSI, complemented by intensive hands-on experience in
designing and building electronic hardware and embedded systems. The programme has been developed
in response to the evolving needs of industry, equipping students with advanced knowledge and skills for
the design of complex electronic and communication systems.
The department is taught by highly qualified and research-active faculty whose expertise spans wireless
and next-generation communications (5G/6G, OTFS, UAV networks), VLSI and microelectronics,
embedded and IoT systems, RF and microwave engineering, signal processing, control, and machine
learning for communications. Every semester integrates project work that combines coding and electronics
skills, supported by well-equipped laboratories and the active mentorship of research scholars.
Vision of the Department
To emerge as a centre of excellence in electronics and communication engineering education and research,
producing globally competent engineers and innovators capable of addressing the technological challenges
of a connected world.
Mission of the Department
1. To provide a rigorous, outcome-based foundation in electronics and communication engineering with
strong hands-on and design exposure.
2. To promote research, innovation, and entrepreneurship in emerging areas such as next-generation
wireless, VLSI, embedded systems, and intelligent electronics.
3. To inculcate professional ethics, lifelong learning, and social responsibility among students.
4. To build strong linkages with industry, research organizations, and the wider community for mutually
beneficial collaboration.
Programme Offered
B.Tech. in Electronics and Communication Engineering — 4 years / 8 semesters, 160 credits, with multiple
entry-exit options, multidisciplinary minors, an Honors track, and a strong project and internship component,
in line with NEP 2020 and the NBA outcome-based education framework.
Credit Structure and Break-up
The programme follows the NEP-based B.Tech. Common Format credit scheme, with a total programme
credit requirement of 160 credits distributed across Lecture (L), Tutorial (T), Practical (P), and Scholarly

3. Outcome-Based Education (OBE) Framework
The B.Tech. (ECE) curriculum is designed and delivered following the Outcome-Based Education (OBE)
philosophy advocated by the National Board of Accreditation (NBA) and consistent with the Washington
Accord. Under OBE, the curriculum, teaching-learning processes, and assessment are aligned to clearly
defined outcomes at three levels — Programme Educational Objectives (PEOs), Programme Outcomes
(POs) with Programme Specific Outcomes (PSOs), and Course Outcomes (COs).

MA-L-01-C Linear Algebra and Matrix Analysis

### Course Code MA-L-01-C Credits 3

Structure (L-T-P-
Category BSC (Basic Science) 2-1-0-0

### S)

Prerequisite Nil Laboratory No
Provides the matrix-algebraic and vector-space foundation underpinning signal processing, communications,
control, machine learning, and numerical computation in electronics engineering.
Course Outcomes (COs)
On successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
CO Statement
CO1 Solve systems of linear equations using Gaussian elimination and matrix inversion.
CO2 Carry out matrix operations including inverses, rank, and determinants.
CO3 Determine eigenvalues and eigenvectors and solve eigenvalue problems.
CO4 Apply matrix algebra to linear transformations and change of basis.
CO5 Demonstrate understanding of inner-product spaces, orthonormalization, and SVD.
Detailed Syllabus (5 Modules)
Mod. Content Hours
Systems of Linear Equations
M1 System of linear equations; Gaussian elimination; inverse and rank of a matrix by 5
elementary transformations; properties and applications of determinants.
Vector Spaces
M2 Vector spaces over real and complex fields; subspaces; linear dependence and 6
independence; spanning set; basis and dimension.
Linear Transformations
M3 Linear transformations and their matrices; kernel and range; change of basis; rank- 6
nullity theorem; matrix representation.
Eigenvalues & Diagonalization
M4 Eigenvalues and eigenvectors; characteristic polynomial; diagonalization of matrices; 6
Cayley-Hamilton theorem and applications.
Inner Product Spaces & SVD
M5 Inner product spaces; norms; orthogonality; Gram-Schmidt orthonormalization; Singular 5
Value Decomposition (SVD) and applications.
